# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tarot students and the esoterically curious — people who want to _learn_ the 78 cards deeply (symbolism, numerology, astrology, qabalah, mythology, elements, herbal/Wiccan lore), one card at a time, rather than have their fortune told. They arrive in a contemplative, exploratory mood, often at night, wanting depth they can trust and a space that feels sacred rather than gimmicky.

## Product Purpose

The Moonlit Grimoire is an interactive study app for the Rider–Waite–Smith tarot: a browsable, filterable library of all 78 cards, each with rich, _sourced_ multi-tradition content, plus a guided Fool's Journey. Success is a reader who can sit with one card, understand its many facets, trust the information (every claim cited), and feel quietly transported. Learning is the soul; the atmosphere serves immersion without getting in the way of reading.

## Brand Personality

Mysterious, reverent, refined. Three words: **sacred, nocturnal, literary**. The voice is that of an old illuminated grimoire reimagined with restraint — evocative but never kitschy, occult but never cheesy. Calm and confident, like a learned guide speaking softly by candlelight.

## Anti-references

- **Distracting display type.** The original Aktura headings called too much attention to themselves. No experimental/ligature-heavy display faces. Headings should be elegant and _calm_ — they frame the content, not perform.
- **Busy, colorful backgrounds.** The original drifting multi-color mist competed with the content. No rainbow gradients, no animated blobs. The backdrop is near-black with at most a subtle dithered/cosmic texture.
- Generic "spooky mystical" kitsch (purple neon, clip-art pentagrams, Papyrus/Trajan clichés), New-Age stock imagery, and anything that reads as AI-generated occult slop.

**Reference inspiration (user-provided):** the _REQUIEM_ poster (dark dithered/halftone ground, elegant ligatured serif used sparingly, arched frames, tiny technical sans labels); the _Tree of Echoes_ witch site (pure-black ground, refined calm serif headings, warm amber glows, small-caps serif lists, gold accents, deeply minimal); and a cosmic _orrery_ chart (near-black with faint gold orbital line-work and a sparse starfield — the "dithered universe").

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the candle; the room is dark around it.** The interface recedes so the reading glows. When in doubt, remove.
2. **Trustworthy by construction.** Every card cites real, resolvable sources; the design treats scholarship as a feature, not a footnote.
3. **Calm hierarchy.** Elegance through restraint — type, space, and a single warm accent do the work; effects are subtle and earned.
4. **Sacred, not silly.** Evoke the mystery traditions with sophistication; never lapse into kitsch.
5. **Quiet motion, full access.** Motion is gentle and optional; everything works keyboard-first and respects reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG-minded: keyboard operability throughout, visible focus, body text ≥4.5:1 against the near-black ground, all motion gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion`, descriptive alt text on all card art, semantic landmarks and headings.
